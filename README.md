## 🥭 About Me

I'm Jesús Huerta Aguilar, a Computer Science graduate from the Faculty of Computer Science (FCC), Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP), currently working as a **Business Intelligence Analyst**.

My professional focus is on transforming data into actionable insights through analytics, reporting, and business intelligence solutions. I work with SQL, Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and Metabase to develop dashboards, optimize reports, validate data quality, and support data-driven decision-making.

I enjoy understanding business processes behind the data and continuously improving my analytical and technical skills to grow as a **Data Analyst**.

## 📊 Interests

I'm passionate about **Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Data Visualization**. My goal is to build a strong career in analytics by combining technical expertise with business understanding to help organizations make better decisions.

I'm particularly interested in:

- Business Intelligence & Data Analytics
- Data Visualization and Dashboard Design
- SQL, Data Modeling, and ETL processes
- KPI development and business performance analysis
- Data quality, documentation, and process improvement
- Continuous learning in Data Engineering and AI
